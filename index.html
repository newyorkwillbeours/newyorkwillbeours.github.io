Format

Ladder system tournament for the next ranked split. Ladder ranking is individual.

Ranking & stats

  • Statlocker tracks overall performance + MVP
  • Tracked and deciding eventual placement: Winrate · Games Played · Points Gained
  • So not just the most unemployed clown wins

Eligibility

  • Only casual / low rank. High elo is allowed, but just to mog, not for prizes, and not allowed to duo/boost low.

Requirements

  • Never ranked / below old Oracle 6 and not an OTP / very washed / below Ritualist 6 (new rank)
  • Minimum 3 characters unlocked
  • Max 67.67% playrate on one character (no OTPs)

Duo rules

  • Duoing allowed within 6 ranks of each other (example: Mystic 1 can duo with Ritualist 1 at most)
  • Ladder ranking stays individual
  • Exception: if you duo 67.67%+ of your games with the same person, you get placed together and split the prize pool

HEROES / WHIZ / CAL / HASH / DOWN will host classes & seminars beforehand for anyone who wants to learn.
